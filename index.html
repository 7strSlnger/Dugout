Sports Packages

Every Game, Every Sport

We carry Comcast, DirecTV, plus the full MLB, NFL, NBA, PGA, and NASCAR packages. If it's on anywhere, it's on here.

14 High-Definition TVs

Screens cover every angle of the bar and dining room. No matter where you sit, you'll have an unobstructed view of the action.

Premium Broadcast Packages

Comcast, DirecTV, MLB Extra Innings, NFL Sunday Ticket, and more — we subscribe to every major package so you never miss a play.

Oregon Video Lottery

Try your luck on our Oregon Lottery terminals — available to players 21 and over during regular bar hours.
